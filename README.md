**SOLMARA** — a sun-soaked open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

Explore the fictional coastal city of Solmara on foot and by car: steal six kinds of vehicles, outrun the police across a 1–5 star wanted level, run 10 story missions for Rosa, take taxi fares, race the beach boulevard, and fight on foot with fists, pistol and rifle. Full HUD with minimap, save/load, synthesized WebAudio sound — all offline, no external assets.

## Run it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or `node serve.ts` and visit http://localhost:8080). WASD to walk and drive, E to enter cars and talk to people, mouse to aim, click to shoot, 1/2/3 to switch weapons, P to pause.
